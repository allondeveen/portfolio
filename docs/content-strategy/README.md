# Content strategy

This section defines the content strategy for the portfolio website. It describes the goals of the website, the intended audience, the organisation of its content, and the role of each type of content in guiding visitors through the site.

The strategy provides a foundation for the functional design and technical design by establishing what content will be published and how it should support the overall objectives of the portfolio.

## Goals

The portfolio website has the following goals:

- Showcase software engineering projects through detailed technical case studies.
- Demonstrate technical expertise by publishing high-quality engineering articles.
- Present previous work in a structured, accessible, and engaging manner.
- Establish credibility as a software engineer through clear explanations of technical challenges, architectural decisions, and trade-offs.
- Enable potential clients, employers, and collaborators to quickly assess relevant experience and expertise.
- Provide clear opportunities for visitors to initiate contact.

## Target audience

The primary audience consists of:

- Recruiters evaluating technical experience and project history.
- Potential employers assessing software engineering skills and engineering maturity.
- Potential clients looking for expertise in front-end architecture, full-stack development, developer tooling, or content management systems.
- Software engineers interested in technical articles, engineering practices, and open-source projects.
