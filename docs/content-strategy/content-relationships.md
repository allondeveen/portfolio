# Content relationships

This document describes the relationships between content types and the cases in which a content type can relate to itself.

## Featured content

Projects and articles can be highlighted on the homepage.

## Related projects

Projects can reference other projects that discuss similar domains or technologies.

Articles can reference projects that provide a case study of applying the technology or topic discussed in the article.

## Related articles

Articles can reference other articles that discuss similar topics.

Projects can reference articles that discuss the broader subject of technologies used in the project.

## Calls to action

Every page ends with a block that directs the visitor to the contact page.
