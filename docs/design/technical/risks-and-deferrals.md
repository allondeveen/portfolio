# Risks and deferrals

## Document status

- **Maturity:** Active; fourteen risks and four deferrals recorded
- **Scope:** Known technical risks, accepted exposure, deliberately deferred work, and the conditions that trigger follow-up or reconsideration.
- **Excludes:** Architectural decision rationale, implementation tasks, and general product backlog items.
- **Prepared ADRs:** No document-level ADR applies. Individual entries link to relevant ADRs when recorded.
- **Related functional design:** No document-level relationship applies. Individual entries link to relevant requirements when recorded.

## Purpose

This document tracks known technical risks and work that has been deliberately deferred. Consequential technical decisions are recorded separately in the [architecture decision records](decisions/README.md).

| RISK-006 | [Uploaded media is publicly addressable](website-delivery.md#media-delivery-boundary) through predictable media namespaces | An accidentally uploaded confidential file can be accessed without website or CMS authorization | Public media is an explicit delivery boundary, while sensitive data is prohibited from the public website and remains behind the CMS; only images and downloads intended for public content should use the public media storage | Accepted only for media intended to be public | Content management and security | Reconsider if private media is required or if sensitive material is uploaded to the public bucket | [ADR 0006](decisions/0006-use-cloudflare-r2-for-object-storage.md) |
| RISK-007 | [Publication invalidation depends on complete dependency mapping and successful Cloudflare purges](website-delivery.md#caching-and-invalidation) | A missed URL, incorrect cache identity, partial purge, or purge failure can leave stale pages, overviews, related-content blocks, redirects, forms, or shared content visible without a bounded maximum staleness; collection-level article and project purges can also cause cache churn | ADR 0008 defines URL-based cache identity and cumulative targeted invalidation; article and project changes purge their collection detail pages, overview variants, and the homepage; broad shared content purges the website HTML cache; purge failures are logged and recovered manually through the Cloudflare dashboard; uncached endpoints remain excluded; cache correctness and TTFB are monitored | Accepted for the portfolio's publication volume and operational model | Content delivery and publishing | Reconsider after repeated purge failures, missed dependencies, unacceptable stale content, excessive collection-level cache churn, or manual recovery becoming burdensome | [ADR 0008](decisions/0008-use-cloudflare-cdn-caching-with-targeted-publication-invalidation.md) |
| RISK-008 | [The accepted tRPC boundary tightly couples the website and CMS to shared TypeScript contracts and coordinated releases](decisions/0007-use-trpc-for-website-to-cms-data-access.md#deployment-compatibility) | Independent deployment or an incompatible contract change could cause failed content, preview, or form requests | Producer, consumer, and shared domain types change in one revision; maintenance mode isolates mixed deployments; both Workers roll back together; runtime boundary validation remains required | Accepted for the monorepo and coordinated-release model | CMS and website integration | Reconsider if either Worker must deploy independently, another-language clients are added, or repeated compatibility failures occur | [ADR 0007](decisions/0007-use-trpc-for-website-to-cms-data-access.md) |
| RISK-009 | [A shared preview token can leak or be consumed by an unintended request](website-delivery.md#preview-delivery), and an incorrect binding or reference-scope implementation could expose unpublished content | The intended recipient may be unable to view the preview, or a leaked or incorrectly validated token may disclose its bound draft and referenced unpublished content | Tokens expire after one hour; bind one item and saved revision to a normalized website URL; authorize only references reached from that revision; and are validated and atomically consumed by the CMS during retrieval. A separate CMS JWT protects the service boundary, preview responses are uncached, and invalid, expired, consumed, or mismatched links expose only public content | Accepted for single-recipient shared previews under ADR 0010 | Preview delivery and security | Reconsider after unintended consumption, a normalization mismatch, suspected unpublished-content disclosure, or a requirement for multi-recipient or reusable previews | [ADR 0010](decisions/0010-use-single-use-tokens-for-shared-previews.md) |
| RISK-010 | [AI-search output and operations combine probabilistic retrieval and generation with eventually consistent derived data](decisions/0009-use-turso-backed-rag-for-ai-search.md) | Hallucinated or unsupported answers, prompt injection, invalid content blocks, privacy leakage, excessive cost, stale or deleted retrieval, identity collisions, synchronization races, or provider failures could harm visitors or operations | Only published projects, articles, and the contact page are eligible; stale jobs cannot overwrite newer generations; four-attempt jobs and complete-source reconciliation repair failures; child-level RRF, bounded context, and minimum evidence constrain generation; cryptographic IP identity enforces sessions and rate limits; dependency, streaming, timeout, and invalid-output failures use defined degraded states; interaction content is excluded from logs | Accepted under the bounded RAG, validation, privacy, and degraded-service rules in ADR 0009 | AI search, security, privacy, and operations | Revisit if freshness, retrieval recall, grounding, identity, safety, latency, cost, or provider constraints fail the ADR's acceptance criteria | [ADR 0009](decisions/0009-use-turso-backed-rag-for-ai-search.md) |

| RISK-012 | [Missing, deleted, or unsupported referenced content can be omitted silently](website-delivery.md#failure-and-degraded-behaviour), and editors are allowed to remove media that is still in use | Published pages may become incomplete, lose navigation or forms, display missing media, or conceal a block-contract incompatibility without failing the whole page | Missing media shows alternative text; missing menu and form blocks and unknown block types are omitted; required-header failure shows the error page; editor warnings, previews, and content validation provide earlier detection | Accepted for graceful degradation, subject to editorial verification and DEF-002 | Content modelling, editing, mapping, and rendering | Reconsider after repeated silent omissions, a block-contract migration, or implementation of deletion and restoration workflows | [ADR 0002](decisions/0002-use-payload-cms.md), [ADR 0007](decisions/0007-use-trpc-for-website-to-cms-data-access.md), [ADR 0008](decisions/0008-use-cloudflare-cdn-caching-with-targeted-publication-invalidation.md) |
| RISK-013 | [Accessibility verification is primarily manual](quality-and-safety.md#accessibility), and automated findings do not necessarily block a release | Regressions may reach production or editor-authored content may fail WCAG 2.2 AA despite renderer safeguards | The shared renderer owns enforceable requirements; editors own semantic content quality; manual verification is required and developer tools and Lighthouse may supplement it | Accepted with manual verification while automated release blocking remains conditional under DEF-004 | Accessibility, content editing, and release verification | Reconsider when project time permits or after recurring preventable accessibility regressions | None |
| RISK-014 | [SEO and crawler verification checks existence rather than semantic validity](quality-and-safety.md#seo-and-structured-data), while crawler access depends partly on provider classification | Incorrect structured data or metadata can reduce discoverability, and legitimate automation may be blocked while unwanted crawlers may still be allowed | The CMS reports missing required values; editors own semantic quality; canonical URLs, indexability, social metadata, sitemap inclusion, and article markup are required; Cloudflare allows recognised Search and Agent traffic and limits Training or unwanted crawling | Accepted with editor review and Cloudflare enforcement | Content editing, SEO, and edge security | Reconsider after search-engine validation errors, material discoverability loss, or crawler misclassification | None |
