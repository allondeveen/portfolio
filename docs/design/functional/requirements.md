# Requirements

These requirements apply to the system as a whole.

## Functional requirements

- The system shall distinguish between draft and published content.
- The system shall allow composition from reusable content blocks.
- The system shall render only published content on the public website.
- The system shall provide visitors with a means of contacting the website owner.

## Non-functional requirements

### Accessibility

- Images shall have appropriate alternative text.
- Text, foreground content, and backgrounds shall meet WCAG 2.2 AA colour-contrast requirements.
- All interactive elements shall be fully operable using only a keyboard.
- The website shall be compatible with common assistive technologies, including screen readers.

### Responsiveness

- The website shall be usable on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
- The layout shall adapt to different viewport sizes without loss of functionality.

### Search-engine optimisation

- Every page shall expose suitable search-engine metadata.
- Every page shall have a unique title.
